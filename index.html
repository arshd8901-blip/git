Safety comes first

Real jobs never ask you to pay.

Red flags: walk away if a "job" …
  • asks for an upfront fee, "training" payment or equipment purchase
  • sends you a check and asks you to deposit it and send money back
  • asks for gift cards, crypto or payment apps
  • wants your bank login, password, or SSN before you've actually been hired
  • offers very high pay for easy work, or hires without any conversation
  • only talks over text or chat apps and uses a free email address instead of the company's
How Jobly protects you
  • Every employer listing is reviewed by a person before it goes live.
  • Each listing states its minimum hiring age and pay.
  • A "Verified" badge means we confirmed the business is real. Listings without it say "Not yet verified".
  • A "Report this listing" form is on every listing.
  • Tell a parent or trusted adult about interviews, and meet in public or at the workplace.

Scammed? Report it at reportfraud.ftc.gov and tell a trusted adult.

For parents & guardians

What Jobly does and doesn't do with your teen's information.

What we collect
  • An email address, only if your teen makes an account.
  • Their resume, availability and application tracker, only if they sign in. Otherwise it stays on their device.
  • An optional profile (a name they type, general area, short bio, interests), only if they turn it on.
  • No home address, birthday, ID number or photos.
What we don't do
  • We don't sell data. A teen's information is private unless they choose to make a profile and switch it on. Then only employers we've verified see the few details they chose.
  • Employers can't message teens directly. They send a request and your teen decides whether to answer. Contact details are shared only if your teen accepts.
  • We never ask teens to pay for anything.

Work rules for under-18 workers (work permits, hours, tasks) vary by state. Check your state's labor agency. To ask about or delete your teen's data, use the Contact page.

      Privacy

      • Jobly is for people aged 16–18.
      • We ask only for an email address to create an account. We don't ask for your home address, birthday, or any ID number.
      • Without an account, your resume, availability and tracker stay on your own device. If you sign in, they're saved to your account so they work on other devices, and only you can read them.
      • We don't sell your information. Employers can't see anything about you unless you make a profile and switch it on. Then only employers we've verified can see what you chose to show: the name you typed, a general area, a short bio, your interests, when you're free, and a resume if you share one. There's no photo, school, address, phone number or email.
      • Employers can't message you directly. They send a request through Jobly and you decide. Your email is shared with an employer only if you accept their request, and you can block, decline, switch your profile off or delete it at any time.
      • If you have a profile, we may email your account address when an employer sends you a request. The email never includes their message or any contact details, and you can turn it off on your profile page. We also email employers about decisions on their listings and requests.
      • Reports and messages you send us are stored so we can respond to them.
      • You can erase your saved data at any time with "Erase my saved data". To delete your account, use the Contact page and choose "Delete my data". Parents or guardians may also contact us.

      Terms

      • Jobly doesn't charge job seekers and will never ask you to pay to get a job.
      • Employers must give accurate information, follow child-labor laws, and never charge applicants. We may reject or remove any listing.
      • Child-labor rules vary by state and job type. Check your state's labor agency or the U.S. Department of Labor before accepting work.
      • Be honest in your resume and applications, and report suspicious listings.
      • Employers who ask to see profiles must be approved by us, may use profiles only to offer real jobs that follow child-labor laws, and must not ask teens for personal details, photos or money, or move them to other apps. We can remove any profile or employer at any time.
